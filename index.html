FAQ

Questions, answered.

Missing something? Send us a message.

Do I need an account to try it?
No. Open the builder and start building right away. You only need a free account when you want to record. Your first 3 clips are free, no credit card required.
What does Pro cost?
14 € a month, or 8.25 € a month on the yearly plan (99 € billed once a year). Both include unlimited clips without a watermark, any background colour and per-hit speed and size changes. You can cancel anytime.
What happens after my 3 free clips?
You can keep building and previewing battles, but recording is locked until you upgrade to Pro. Pro gives you unlimited clips without a watermark for 14 € a month, and you can cancel anytime.
Can I use the clips commercially?
Yes. Anything you export is yours to post, monetize or use commercially.
Does it work on mobile?
Yes, the builder runs in any modern browser. On desktop you get more room to fine-tune settings.
Which browsers support recording?
Recording works best in current versions of Chrome, Edge and Firefox. Clips are converted to MP4 right in your browser.
