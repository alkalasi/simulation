Every battle needs a format

Six ways to turn two balls and a health bar into a video worth watching to the end.

1v1

Duel

Two balls, two health bars, one arena. Every collision chips away at HP until a single winner is left standing.

Odds Coming Soon

Underdog

Give one ball a size and damage disadvantage on purpose. The bigger the gap, the more satisfying the comeback.

Chaos

Free-For-All

Add up to six balls to one arena. Health bars stack on screen and the field thins out hit by hit.

Bracket Coming Soon

Tournament

Run a string of duels back to back and carry the winner's damaged health bar into the next round.

Boss

Boss Fight

One oversized ball with a health bar in the thousands against a swarm of small ones chipping it down.

Roulette Coming Soon

Damage Roulette

Hit damage is randomized within a range you set, so even a rematch with the same names never plays out the same way.

From idea to export in four steps

No design skills needed. The defaults already look like a finished clip.

1

Name your fighters

Type two names — a rivalry, two friends, two brands — anything that gives the fight a reason to matter.

2

Set colors & damage

Pick each ball's color, size, and hit damage. Even the odds, or don't — that's the story you're telling.

3

Run the battle

Press start and watch the health bars drain in real time. Restart as many times as it takes to get a clean finish.

4

Export & post

Render an MP4 in vertical 1080×1920, download it, and post — no watermark, no re-encoding.

Built to be tweaked

Every fight is made of small settings. Change one and the whole clip feels different.

🩸

Multi-stage health bars

Segment each ball's HP into stages so a fight has visible turning points, not just one long bar draining.

💥

Hit & damage counters

A running counter tracks hits landed and total damage dealt, live on screen as the fight plays.

🎨

Custom names & colors

Every fighter gets a name label and a color, so viewers always know who's winning at a glance.

🖼️

Custom ball image

Drop in a logo, a face, or an emoji instead of a plain color — great for brand or fan-vs-fan clips.

🔊

Impact sound

Each hit triggers a sound cue, so the fight has an audio rhythm even before you add music.

🎬

Vertical export

Render straight to 1080×1920 MP4, 10–120 seconds, ready to drop into TikTok, Reels, or Shorts.
